title: Children's Liturgy of the Word
updated: 2026-10-02
---
The Children’s Liturgy of the Word provides children, ages 4 – 14, with an opportunity to reflect on the Sunday Scripture readings at their level of understanding. Parishioners serve as teams for setting up, leading the children in sharing, reading, and music. This takes place during the 9:30 a.m. Mass.
