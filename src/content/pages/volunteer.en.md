"With the desire to glorify God and make Him known, I pledge to serve with utmost humility through my ministries at Saint Maria Goretti Parish. So, help me God."

Thank you for answering the call to volunteer at St. Maria Goretti. We ask all new volunteers to complete the following:

1. Fill out these two forms: [Volunteer Form](/uploads/docs/volunteer-application1-pdf.pdf) and Volunteer Activity Waiver Form ([English](/uploads/docs/volunteer-activity-waiver-2023-pdf.pdf) | [Spanish](/uploads/docs/volunteer-waiver-covid-19-spanish-pdf.pdf) | [Vietnamese](/uploads/docs/volunteer-waiver-covid-19-vietnamese-pdf.pdf)), then drop it off at the parish office. The office manager will reach out to you about fingerprinting.

2. Complete the mandatory [Safe Environment Training](https://www.virtusonline.org/virtus/) and email your certificate to ttran@smgsj.org. The training is available in English, Spanish, Vietnamese, and Korean. Registration instructions: [English](https://www.dsj.org/wp-content/uploads/VIRTUS-Registration-Instructions-Jan-2021-1.pdf) and [Spanish](https://www.dsj.org/wp-content/uploads/VIRTUS-Registration-Instructions-Jan-2021_SP-1.pdf); [accessing your certificate](https://www.dsj.org/wp-content/uploads/VIRTUS-Accessing-Training-Certificate-Jan-2021.pdf); [re-certification](https://www.dsj.org/wp-content/uploads/VIRTUS-Recertification-Instructions-Jan-2021.pdf).

Any questions about these requirements, please contact Tam Tran at 408-363-2300 ext. 1011 or email ttran@smgsj.org.
