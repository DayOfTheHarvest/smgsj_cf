## ILM
*Knowledge | Spirituality | Leadership*  

The Institute for Leadership in Ministry is a three-year program which provides formation in Catholic theology, pastoral ministry and spirituality; and education and training in leadership.  
Our goal is to prepare competent ministerial leaders to serve throughout the Diocese of San Jose.

Here are some qualities of the ideal candidate:

- a call to the ministry of leadership within the Catholic community
- demonstrated or potential leadership ability
- current participation in pastoral ministry
- prior formation in the faith
- willingness to commit to 10-12 hours per month for classes
- ability to complete the reading and writing assignments
- ability to devote additional hours outside of class for assignments.

**Admissions criteria**: The mission of the Institute is the formation of competent leaders. The ideal candidate being considered for the Institute should have:

- a *call to the ministry of leadership* within the Catholic community
- *demonstrated or potential leadership* ability
- *participation* in some form of pastoral ministry
- a *willingness to commit* to 10–12 hours per month for classes and seminars
- the *ability to devote additional hours* outside of class for reading and writing papers.

### Admissions process

All prospective students must be recommended by their pastor. When invited to do so, candidate will complete the application form.

### Candidates

- **Complete** the Application Form including the letter of intent.
- **Submit** the Application Form to your pastor for review.
- **Await** being contacted by the Director and advised of their acceptance upon pastor approval and receipt of all forms.

### Pastor

- **Review** candidate’s application forms.
- **Select** candidates to recommend. (Admissions criteria)
- **Complete** the ILM Recommendation Form for each candidate being recommended.
- **Send** the Recommendation Form with the candidate’s Application Form to the Director of the Institute

Note: Due to limited space, when a class is full, the candidate will be placed on a waiting list and is guaranteed admission the following year.

### Please see the [Admissions Page](https://www.dsj.org/evangelization/institute-for-leadership-in-ministry/ilm-admissions/) for more information on the process for the current academic year. Classes begin mid-September.
