  body: "## ILM\n*Knowledge | Spirituality | Leadership*  \n\nThe Institute for Leadership in Ministry is a three-year program which provides formation in Catholic theology, pastoral ministry and spirituality; and education and training in leadership.  \nOur goal is to prepare competent ministerial leaders to serve throughout the Diocese of San Jose.\n\nHere are some qualities of the ideal candidate:\n\n- a call to the ministry of leadership within the Catholic community\n- demonstrated or potential leadership ability\n- current participation in pastoral ministry\n- prior formation in the faith\n- willingness to commit to 10-12 hours per month for classes\n- ability to complete the reading and writing assignments\n- ability to devote additional hours outside of class for assignments.\n\n**Admissions criteria**: The mission of the Institute is the formation of competent leaders. The ideal candidate being considered for the Institute should have:\n\n- a *call to the ministry of leadership*\
    \ within the Catholic community\n- *demonstrated or potential leadership* ability\n- *participation* in some form of pastoral ministry\n- a *willingness to commit* to 10–12 hours per month for classes and seminars\n- the *ability to devote additional hours* outside of class for reading and writing papers.\n\n### Admissions process\n\nAll prospective students must be recommended by their pastor. When invited to do so, candidate will complete the application form.\n\n### Candidates\n\n- **Complete** the Application Form including the letter of intent.\n- **Submit** the Application Form to your pastor for review.\n- **Await** being contacted by the Director and advised of their acceptance upon pastor approval and receipt of all forms.\n\n### Pastor\n\n- **Review** candidate’s application forms.\n- **Select** candidates to recommend. (Admissions criteria)\n- **Complete** the ILM Recommendation Form for each candidate being recommended.\n- **Send** the Recommendation Form with the candidate’s\
    \ Application Form to the Director of the Institute\n\nNote: Due to limited space, when a class is full, the candidate will be placed on a waiting list and is guaranteed admission the following year.\n\n### Please see the [Admissions Page](https://www.dsj.org/evangelization/institute-for-leadership-in-ministry/ilm-admissions/) for more information on the process for the current academic year. Classes begin mid-September."
