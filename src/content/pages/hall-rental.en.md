St. Maria Goretti Vietnamese Martyrs Hall Rental Request Form (max capacity: 80 dining / 120 assembly).

Please provide: event name, event date, start time including setup/decoration, end time including cleanup (no event beyond 10 PM), number of attendance, contact name, phone, and email address.

A $500 deposit is required to hold the reservation if the space is available. Submitting a request does not guarantee a reservation. Our office processes requests first come, first served. All fees are subject to change without notice. We reserve the right to accept or deny any requests.

To request the hall, see [Request Forms](/en/requests/) or call [408-363-2300](tel:4083632300).
